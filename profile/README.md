# DinnerDibs

Community-based platform for homemade foods and local cooks. Members can buy and sell homemade food at a desired and on-demand price.

DinnerDibs is a community-driven digital marketplace designed to facilitate the discovery, exchange, and distribution of homemade food between independent home cooks and local consumers. The platform seeks to address the structural inefficiencies and accessibility limitations present in existing food delivery ecosystems by removing intermediaries and enabling direct, peer-to-peer interaction between buyers and sellers. Unlike traditional restaurant-based platforms, DinnerDibs is intentionally scoped to exclude large-scale commercial food businesses, instead focusing on individuals and small-scale home-based food entrepreneurs.
